The current Tokyu plan is dated 21 August 2026, but its embedded exit-direction guide is dated 30 August 2024. A5/A6 directional associations retain the older date. Facility webpages without publication dates carry the check date, not an invented publication date.

The remaining selected exit badges are inventory items from the station diagram. An empty destinations list means mapping pending, not proof that an exit has no connection. Lines represent association only: they are not validated walkable paths and do not imply that a displayed lift or stair participates in a navigable route.
